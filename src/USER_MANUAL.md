## 3. Settlement Valuations

### How the Valuation Model Works

**Input Data:**
- Case type (professional negligence, etc.)
- Breach type (competence, conduct, etc.)
- Evidence strength (weak, moderate, strong, critical)
- Estimated damages
- Jurisdiction (England, Scotland, Wales, NI)

**Process:**
- System compares your case to 10,000+ historical cases
- Identifies 5-10 most similar precedents
- Calculates average settlement from comparable cases
- Adjusts for your case's specific factors
- Returns estimate + confidence score

**Output:**
- **Predicted settlement:** Most likely value (±5%)
- **Range:** 25th-75th percentile (low/high estimates)
- **Confidence:** 0-100% (higher = more data available)
- **Comparable cases:** Show 3-5 similar historical settlements

### Using Valuations Strategically

**For Initial Assessment:**
- Get quick ballpark within first week
- Share with client to set expectations

**For Negotiation:**
- Cite "AI predicted £65K" as evidence-backed position
- Reference comparable cases shown in analysis

**For Settlement Decisions:**
- Compare offer against predicted value
- Assess if offer is above/below 75th percentile

**For Case Profitability:**
- Factor into engagement letters
- Estimate potential recovery fees

### Valuation Accuracy

- 85% of predictions fall within ±5% of actual settlement
- Accuracy improves as you add more evidence
- Confidence score tells you how reliable the estimate is
- Benchmarks updated monthly with new case law

## 5. RICS Compliance

### Understanding RICS Breach Scoring

**Breach Probability (0-100%):**
- 0-20%: Low risk
- 21-50%: Medium risk
- 51-80%: High risk
- 81-100%: Very high risk

**Severity Levels:**
- **Minor:** Technical breach, minimal damage, easy remedy
- **Moderate:** Clear breach, some damages, fixable
- **Severe:** Serious breach, significant damages
- **Critical:** Egregious breach, major damages, discipline likely

### What Gets Flagged

**System detects:**
- Competence failures (breach of standard of care)
- Professional conduct violations (misleading clients, conflicts)
- Conflicts of interest (representing both sides, etc.)
- Client care failures (no survey agreed in writing, etc.)
- Complaints handling (ignoring complaints, etc.)
- Information control (not keeping secrets, etc.)
- Gatekeeping (not checking legitimacy of buyer, etc.)
- Harassment (discriminatory language, etc.)

### Taking Action on Flags

**If Red (High Risk):**
1. Review mitigation recommendations
2. Consider additional expert report
3. Document all remediation steps
4. May want to seek insurance/counsel advice

**If Amber (Medium Risk):**
1. Monitor closely as case develops
2. Add evidence that mitigates the breach
3. Prepare response to breach allegation
4. Consider early settlement vs. litigation cost

**If Green (Low Risk):**
1. Continue normal case progression
2. Still document compliance
3. Re-check after new evidence uploaded

### Downloading Compliance Report

1. Go to "RICS Assessment" tab
2. Click "Download Compliance Report"
3. PDF generated with:
   - Breach probability breakdown
   - Specific rules violated
   - Risk factors identified
   - Recommended mitigations
4. Share with partners/compliance team

## 7. Witness Collaboration

### Inviting a Witness

**Steps:**
1. Click "Witnesses" tab in case
2. Click "+ Invite Witness"
3. Enter:
   - Witness name (e.g., "Dr. James Smith")
   - Email address

5. Click "Send Invitation"

**What Witness Receives:**
- Email with subject line: "[Case] needs your input"

- No account creation needed
- Can view case overview, timeline, evidence

### What Witnesses Can See

**Witness Portal Shows:**
- Case overview (date, parties, damages)
- Incident timeline
- Uploaded evidence
- Questions/requests for witness input

**Witness Cannot See:**
- Your internal notes
- Financial discussions

**Witness Can:**

- Answer questions you've posed

**You See:**

## 8. Deadline Management

### Setting Up Milestones

**Key Deadlines to Track:**
1. **Limitation Date** (most critical!) — statutory deadline to sue
2. **Court Deadlines** — filing, evidence, hearing dates
3. **Client Contact Due** — regular update required
4. **Settlement Deadline** — offer expires on this date
5. **Custom** — any other important date

**Steps to Add Milestone:**
1. Click case → "Milestones" tab
2. Click "+ Add Milestone"
3. Select type (Limitation Date, Court Deadline, etc.)
4. Enter date
5. (Optional) Add notes (e.g., "Original offer expires")
6. Click "Enable Reminders"

### How Reminders Work

**Automatic Alerts:**
- **30 days before:** Email + in-app notification
- **7 days before:** Email + banner alert
- **1 day before:** Email + red banner on dashboard
- **On the date:** Final reminder email

**You Receive:**
- Email to your registered email
- In-app notification (bell icon)
- Dashboard banner (can't miss it)

### Limitation Dates

**Why Critical:**
- Statutory deadline to start legal claim
- Usually 3-6 years from breach
- Missing it = case dismissed, no recovery possible

**Common Dates:**
- Professional negligence (surveyors): 6 years from breach
- RICS breach: 6 years from discovery (if hidden breach)
- Conveyancing: 3 years from completion (check specific statute)

**Set Immediately:**
- Enter as soon as you calculate it
- Don't wait for evidence
- Never miss this deadline

### Court Deadlines

**Typically:**
- Evidence disclosure deadline
- Expert report deadline
- Pre-trial review date
- Trial date
- Post-judgment actions

**Source:**
- From court orders (enter manually)
- From settlement agreements
- From procedural rules

## 12. FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does settlement prediction take?**
A: 30 seconds after uploading evidence. More data = more accurate.

**Q: Can I edit the AI narrative?**
A: Yes. Download PDF, edit in Word, use as your brief.

**Q: What if I make a mistake when creating a case?**
A: Delete case (in Settings → Cases), start over. No cost.

**Q: Can I downgrade my subscription?**
A: Yes. Go to Settings → Billing → Change Plan. Prorated refund applied.

**Q: What happens if I don't upgrade after trial?**
A: Access freezes after 14 days. Data available for 90 days to download.

**Q: How do I share a case with my barrister?**
A: Invite as witness. They get secure link, no account needed.

**Q: Can I export cases for backup?**
A: Yes. Settings → Data → Download Cases (CSV + documents).

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. ISO 27001 certified, GDPR compliant, UK servers, AES-256 encryption.

**Q: How often are settlement benchmarks updated?**
A: Monthly, with latest case law and settlements.

**Q: Can I generate multiple narratives for the same case?**
A: Yes. Each time you add evidence, regenerate for updated brief.

**Q: What's included in the white-label API?**
A: Settlement valuations, narrative generation, RICS scoring, document analysis, witness invites. (Enterprise only)

### Troubleshooting

**"My narrative didn't generate"**
- Ensure at least 2 incidents created
- Ensure at least 1 document uploaded
- Try again, wait 60 seconds
- Contact support if persists

**"Settlement estimate seems wrong"**
- Check confidence score (low confidence = less reliable)
- Add more evidence documents
- Ensure case type selected correctly
- Compare to "comparable cases" shown

**"Witness invitation not received"**
- Check spam folder
- Confirm email address spelled correctly
- Resend invitation
- Try different email (if available)

**"Can't log in"**
- Reset password (click "Forgot Password")
- Check browser cookies enabled
- Try incognito/private mode
- Clear browser cache
- Contact support

**"My cases disappeared"**
- Check if you're logged in as correct user
- Check if firm was changed in settings
- Cases may be archived (check Archives section)
- Contact support if still missing

**"Documents not uploading"**
- Check file size (<20MB)
- Check file format (PDF, JPG, PNG, etc.)
- Try different browser
- Contact support with error message
