## Upgrade / Downgrade

### How to Upgrade
1. Click "Upgrade Plan" in Settings → Billing
2. Select new tier
3. Confirm charge (prorated for current month)
4. New limits apply immediately
5. Confirmation email sent

### Upgrade Example
- Current: Starter (£49/mo) on day 15 of 30
- Upgrade to: Professional (£149/mo)
- Prorated charge: £100 (for remaining 15 days)
- Total month: £49 + £100 = £149
- Next month: £149 (full Professional)

### How to Downgrade
1. Click "Change Plan" in Settings → Billing
2. Select lower tier
3. Confirm (any credit applied to next invoice)
4. New limits apply end of current billing period
5. Grace period: Access to previous tier features for 24 hours

### Downgrade Example
- Current: Premium (£299/mo) on day 15 of 30
- Downgrade to: Professional (£149/mo)
- Credit issued: £150 (for remaining 15 days at Professional rate)
- Applied to: Next invoice
- Next month: £149 (full Professional)

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: No. All team members share one firm account with role-based access.

**Q: Do team members need individual subscriptions?**
A: No. All seats included in your tier. Unlimited team members in Premium/Enterprise.

**Q: Can I downgrade mid-month?**
A: Yes. Prorated credit applied to next invoice. New limits take effect end of current billing period.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my case limit?**
A: You can't create new cases. Upgrade to higher tier to continue.

**Q: Is there a contract?**
A: No. Month-to-month billing, cancel anytime. Annual plans auto-renew yearly.

**Q: Do you offer education/nonprofit discounts?**
A: Enterprise tier customers can inquire about volume discounts. Contact enterprise@casenarra.co.uk.

**Q: What if I need custom pricing?**
A: Enterprise tier is flexible. Contact enterprise@casenarra.co.uk for volume/usage negotiation.
