# CaseNarrative Getting Started Guide

**Welcome to CaseNarrative!** This guide will get you up and running in 10 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account (1 minute)

1. Visit [casenarra.co.uk](https://casenarra.co.uk)
2. Click **"Start Free Trial"**
3. Enter your firm name and email
4. Choose a case template (or start blank)
5. Confirm your email

**Done!** You're now in CaseNarrative.

---

## Step 2: Explore Your First Case (3 minutes)

### If You Selected a Template
You'll see **Bradley v Belcher** (or another example) pre-loaded with:
- ✅ Complete incident timeline
- ✅ Real communications
- ✅ Uploaded evidence documents
- ✅ Witness records
- ✅ RICS risk analysis

**What to do:**
1. Click on **"Dashboard"** to see the case overview
2. Check out the **"Timeline"** tab to see the incident progression
3. Browse the **"Evidence"** section to see uploaded documents
4. Look at the **"RICS Assessment"** to see the compliance risk score

### If You Started Blank
Click **"Create First Case"** and fill in:
- Case name (e.g., "Smith v ABC Surveyors")
- Case type (professional negligence, RICS breach, etc.)
- Claimant name and firm
- Estimated damages

---

## Step 3: Add Your First Incident (2 minutes)

Incidents are the key events in your case (e.g., "Survey completed," "Defect discovered").

1. Go to **"Incidents"** tab
2. Click **"+ New Incident"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Date:** When did this happen?
   - **Title:** "Initial survey" or "Client complaint"
   - **Description:** What happened?
   - **Type:** Select incident category
4. Click **"Create"**

**Tip:** Add 3-5 incidents to give CaseNarrative context for better analysis.

---

## Step 4: Upload Evidence (2 minutes)

Evidence is anything that supports your case: documents, emails, reports, photos.

1. Go to **"Evidence"** tab
2. Click **"+ Upload Document"**
3. Select a PDF, image, or email
4. Add a title (e.g., "Initial Survey Report")
5. Click **"Upload"**

**CaseNarrative will:**
- Extract text automatically
- Identify key clauses and dates
- Flag potential risks
- Link to relevant incidents

---

## Step 5: Generate Your First AI Narrative (1 minute)

This is the magic. CaseNarrative generates a structured legal brief from your case.

1. Go to **"Case Narrative"** tab
2. Click **"Generate AI Brief"**
3. Wait 30 seconds...
4. You'll see:
   - Executive summary
   - Timeline (incident-by-incident)
   - Breach analysis (RICS violations identified)
   - Damages assessment
   - Settlement recommendation

**Export it:**
- Click **"Download PDF"** to get a court-ready document
- Or share with your client

---

## Step 6: Check RICS Compliance Risk (1 minute)

CaseNarrative automatically scores the breach probability.

1. Go to **"RICS Assessment"** tab
2. You'll see:
   - **Breach Probability:** e.g., "78% likely"
   - **Severity:** High, Medium, Low
   - **Violations:** Specific RICS rules breached
   - **Mitigation:** Recommended actions
3. Click **"View Details"** for full analysis

**Why it matters:**
- Early warning system for compliance issues
- Guides your investigation strategy
- Supports settlement negotiations

---

## Step 7: Invite a Witness (Optional, 2 minutes)

Need input from a surveyor, expert, or other witness?

1. Go to **"Witnesses"** tab
2. Click **"+ Invite Witness"**
3. Enter:
   - Witness name
   - Email address
   - Their role (e.g., "Expert Surveyor")
4. Click **"Send Invitation"**

**What happens:**
- Witness gets an email link
- They can view case details securely
- No external account needed
- You see when they viewed/commented
- Full audit trail kept

---

## Step 8: Set Up Deadline Reminders (1 minute)

Never miss a limitation date again.

1. Go to **"Settings"** → **"Milestones"**
2. Click **"+ Add Milestone"**
3. Select type:
   - Limitation date (most important!)
   - Court deadline
   - Client contact due
   - Settlement deadline
4. Enter the date
5. Click **"Enable Reminders"**

**CaseNarrative will:**
- Alert you 30 days before
- Alert again 7 days before
- Alert again on the day

## Next Steps: Common Workflows

### Workflow 1: Fast Settlement Valuation
1. Upload the survey report + complaint
2. Add 2-3 key incidents
3. Click **"Get Settlement Estimate"**
4. See predicted value in 30 seconds
5. Use to guide negotiation

**Time:** 5 minutes. Value: Often worth £10K-50K+ in better negotiations.

### Workflow 2: RICS Compliance Audit
1. Add all incidents and communications
2. Go to RICS Assessment
3. Review identified violations
4. Download compliance report
5. Share with partners/insurers

**Time:** 10 minutes. Value: Prevents costly discipline actions.

### Workflow 3: Court Documentation
1. Upload all evidence and witness statements
2. Generate AI narrative
3. Download PDF (court-formatted)
4. Add your own annotations if needed
5. Submit to court

**Time:** 15 minutes. Value: Saves 4+ hours of manual writing.

## Tips & Tricks

### 💡 Tip 1: Use Templates
If your firm handles similar case types (e.g., always professional negligence), use templates. Saves 30 mins per case.

### 💡 Tip 2: Tag Evidence
Tag documents with keywords (e.g., "breach evidence," "mitigating factor") for easier searching later.

### 💡 Tip 3: Add Communications
Email chains and letters are valuable. Upload them. CaseNarrative extracts tone and concerning language automatically.

### 💡 Tip 4: Check Risk Alerts
Red banner on your case? That's CaseNarrative flagging a compliance issue. Act on it.

### 💡 Tip 5: Share Narratives with Clients
Generate a narrative and share the PDF with your client for feedback. They'll be impressed.
